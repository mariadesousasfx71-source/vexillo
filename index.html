About Vexillo

Vexillo comes from the Latin vexillum, the cloth banner carried by Roman soldiers, and the root of vexillology, the study of flags.

Every flag is a small story of a nation: its history, its hopes and its people. Vexillo lets you search any country, see its flag, and discover the facts behind it.
